A long-only systematic breakout strategy for crypto perpetual futures. A higher-timeframe channel first decides whether the market is trending or ranging; lower-timeframe breakout signals are allowed to fire only when direction is confirmed. Method

Six gates, every one of them rejects orders

A signal must clear market regime, breakout trigger, entry timing, signal quality, event avoidance and capital constraints, in that order. Any gate that fails leaves a decision record with its reason and no position. The system therefore sits flat for long stretches in ranging markets.

01 / Regime

Higher timeframe sets direction

A breakout channel and a volatility channel on the higher timeframe jointly classify the market as trending or ranging. When it reads as ranging, every lower-timeframe breakout is rejected outright.

02 / Trigger

Session rails breakout

After each session opens, historical range produces a pair of buy and sell rails fixed for the day. Only a close beyond a rail counts as a valid breakout; an intrabar wick does not.

03 / Timing

Pullback confirmation

A confirmed breakout is not chased. Price must pull back into a tolerance band around a short-period moving average before an order goes out. Breakouts that stay outside the band are abandoned rather than filled at a worse price.

04 / Worth the bet?

Multi-factor quality score

Trend conviction, breakout depth, pullback precision and volume expansion combine into a 0–1 quality score. Below threshold, no position is opened. The score and each component are stored with the decision.

05 / Event avoidance

Macro blackout window

Inside a fixed window before high-impact events such as Federal Reserve rate decisions, the system neither opens new positions nor adds to existing ones. The calendar is built in and warns before it runs out.

06 / How much

Risk budget sets size

The stop location is fixed first, then contract count is derived from how much risk the trade is allowed to carry — not the other way round. Per-trade risk, per-position risk, per-instrument margin share and total exposure each clip the size in turn.

Engineering

The strategy is half of it; staying alive is the other half

Live trading and backtesting run the same engine code, and one configuration both replays history and drives the live account. Backtest conclusions can therefore be verified bar by bar against live behaviour.

Full decision audit replayable

Not just fills. Every bar and every instrument writes a decision record with each quality-score component and the reason for rejection. "Why was there no entry?" can always be answered down to the specific gate.

Market data resilience self-healing

A dropped feed reconnects and backfills the missing bars. An outage past a threshold trips a circuit breaker that halts new entries. Brief flaps are debounced, so they do not generate alert noise.

Ledger reconciliation every 15s

While flat, book equity is continuously compared against the exchange balance and any mismatch alerts immediately. A missed fill, fee or funding charge surfaces within a minute, not at month-end.

Stops live at the exchange process-independent

The initial stop rests as an order at the exchange, so a crash, a network loss or a restart does not affect it. After a restart the system adopts existing positions without duplicating orders.

Hard capital limits layered

Per-trade risk, per-position risk, per-instrument margin share and total exposure clip contract count before the order is sent. When a limit binds, the reason is recorded rather than the size being silently shrunk.

Parameter changes leave a trail auditable

Every configuration change ships with its evidence: before-and-after backtests, the sample window and the basis for the decision, all committed to version control. Nothing changes on a hunch.

Profit is highly concentrated — this is the thing to know

The best two months together account for 70.3% of all profit, and 4 of the remaining 12 months were negative. The pattern repeats across instruments: the single largest contributor produced 34.6% of total profit, while the bottom two were negative.

Returns come from a handful of strong trending regimes, not from steady daily accumulation. In ranging markets the system stays flat for long stretches — live, it once went six consecutive days without a single entry. Any arithmetic that extrapolates the annualized figure down to a month or a week is wrong.
